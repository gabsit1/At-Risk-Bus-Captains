Task      : predict which of 1,544 bus captains had an accident (161 did = 10.4%).
Test      : 3x repeated 5-fold cross-validation; every score is from captains the model had not seen.
Models    : 8 trained models + 4 simple baselines (random guessing = the floor).
Label set : 'all' accidents.

HOW TO READ THE NUMBERS
  ROC-AUC      : chance the model ranks an accident captain above a non-accident one. 0.50 = guessing.
  PR-AUC       : precision-recall quality. Guessing scores 0.104 (the accident rate).
  Accuracy     : share of captains classified correctly. 'Predict nobody' already gets 89.6%!
  Precision    : of the captains FLAGGED as at-risk, the share who really had an accident.
  Recall       : of all accident captains, the share that were flagged.
  Lift         : precision divided by the overall accident rate (1.0x = no better than random).
